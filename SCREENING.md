# SCREENING — Funniest One-Line Out-of-Office Reply

Judge criteria, in order (from RULES.txt):
1. Is a one-line out-of-office reply, the sort a person sets before going away (hard gate).
2. Laugh lands on the first read, no setup, nothing to explain afterwards.
3. Still reads like a real away message a person would be glad to switch on, not a notice with a joke bolted on.
4. Joke is fresh: a turn on the usual away message, NOT the same beach, inbox or limited-email lines.
5. Short enough to take in at a glance, every word earning its place.

Banned/stock angles (criterion 4 names them): beach, inbox, limited email access. None used below.
